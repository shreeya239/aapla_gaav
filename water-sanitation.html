Drinking Water Security in Valivade

Valivade benefits from its geographical proximity to the fertile Panchganga river basin, which ensures rich groundwater recharge. Under the flagship Jal Jeevan Mission (Har Ghar Jal), Valivade Gram Panchayat has established an elevated storage reservoir (ESR) and underground distribution lines to deliver potable water directly to households.

The village holds a proud Open Defecation Free (ODF) certification under the Swachh Bharat Mission (Gramin), with individual household sanitary latrines (IHHL) across all 332 residential households (Census 2011 baseline).
