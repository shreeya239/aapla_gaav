Health & Family Welfare Sector

Primary healthcare delivery, immunization, maternal health & Ayushman Bharat coverage in Valivade

CEP Health Index: 74%
Primary Healthcare

Valivade Health Sub-Centre (आरोग्य उपकेंद्र)

The village is served by the Government Health Sub-Centre, Valivade, which operates under the administrative supervision of the Primary Health Centre (PHC) cluster in Karvir. The sub-centre caters to the regular health needs of Valivade's 1,668 population (Census 2011 reference), providing maternal and child healthcare, fever clinics, and immunization drives.

Emergency cases and complex medical treatments are referred to Chhatrapati Pramila Raje (CPR) Civil Hospital, Kolhapur or empanelled Ayushman Bharat tertiary hospitals located within 15–20 minutes travel time.

Public Health Services & Programs

💉

Mission Indradhanush Immunization

बाल लसीकरण मोहीम • 100% Target

Routine vaccines for all infants and children under 5 years covering Polio, BCG, Pentavalent, Rotavirus, MR, and DPT boosters. Administered weekly by the ANM.

Free of Cost
🤰

Maternal Health & PMSMA

मातृ व बाल संगोपन • Pradhan Mantri Matritva

Antenatal check-ups (ANC), IFA tablets distribution, high-risk pregnancy screening, and institutional delivery linkage under Janani Suraksha Yojana (JSY).

Govt Supported
🪪

Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY)

आयुष्मान भारत गोल्डन कार्ड • ₹5 Lakh Cover

Annual cashless health coverage of up to ₹5,00,000 per family for secondary and tertiary hospitalization across empanelled private and government hospitals.

E-Card Assistance Available

Non-Communicable Disease (NCD) & Preventive Care

Hypertension & Diabetes Screening

Free BP and blood sugar testing for all village adults aged 30+ at the Sub-Centre.

Vector-Borne Disease Control

Periodic fogging, abatement of standing water, and zero-dengue surveillance drives by Panchayat staff.

ASHA Health Workers Cadre

Door-to-door community health volunteers conducting household surveys and providing first aid.
