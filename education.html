Sector Overview

Educational Landscape of Valivade

The village of Valivade (वळिवडे) is served by the Zilla Parishad Primary School, Valivade, which imparts foundational education from Grade 1 through 7. Early childhood care and nutritional support are provided by two integrated Anganwadi Centers under the ICDS (Integrated Child Development Services) framework.

For secondary, higher secondary, and polytechnic studies, students utilize convenient transport links to educational institutions in nearby Karvir and Kolhapur city clusters (approximately 8–10 km away).

Village Educational Institutions

🏫

Zilla Parishad Primary School

जि. प. प्राथमिक शाळा, वळिवडे • U-DISE Verified

State-board recognized co-educational primary school offering Marathi medium instruction from Std 1 to 7. Equipped with qualified ZP teachers and activity-based learning.

  • Daily hot cooked Mid-Day Meal (PM POSHAN)
  • Free uniforms and state textbooks
  • Separate boy and girl sanitation facilities
👶

Anganwadi Center 1 & 2

अंगणवाडी केंद्र • ICDS Karvir Project

Early childhood care, pre-primary non-formal education for children aged 3–6 years, and supplementary nutrition for pregnant women and lactating mothers.

  • Routine child growth monitoring (WHO charts)
  • Immunization linkage with ASHA & ANM
  • Pre-school kit and interactive toys
💻

Digital Classroom & Library

ई-लर्निंग व वाचनालय • Gram Panchayat Initiative

Community e-learning unit and reading library established under village development funds to enhance digital literacy and competitive exam preparation.

  • Broadband internet & smart TV display
  • Curated Marathi & English story books
  • Basic computer training for youth

Student Scholarships & Welfare Schemes

Pre-Matric Scholarship

For SC/ST/OBC students studying in Std 1 to 10 to support education expenses and prevent dropouts.

Savitribai Phule Attendance Scheme

Special financial incentive for girl students to maintain 75%+ annual attendance in primary schools.

Mid-Day Meal (PM POSHAN)

Nutritious balanced lunch served daily with iron and folic acid supplementation.
