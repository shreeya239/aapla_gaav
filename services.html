Citizen Services & e-Certificates

Official Gram Panchayat Digital Public Services • Right to Public Services Act (RTS)

Right to Public Services (RTS) Guarantee:

All citizen certificates are processed digitally by Valivade Gram Panchayat within 3 to 7 working days. No physical visit needed for verified residents.

Download Offline PDF Forms
👶
3 Working Days

Birth Certificate (जन्म दाखला)

Certified birth certificate copy from Gram Panchayat register under Registration of Births and Deaths Act.

Required: Hospital discharge card, Parents Aadhaar card & marriage certificate.
Official Fee: ₹20 per certified copy.
📜
3 Working Days

Death Certificate (मृत्यू दाखला)

Official registration and certified copy of death entry from Valivade Gram Panchayat vital records.

Required: Doctor certificate / Cremation ground slip, Deceased Aadhaar card.
Official Fee: ₹20 per certified copy.
🏡
2 Working Days

Residence Certificate (रहिवासी दाखला)

Verification certificate confirming permanent or temporary residency within Valivade village limits.

Required: Aadhaar Card, Electricity Bill / Ration card, Voter ID.
Official Fee: ₹10.
📑
Same Day / 24h

Assessment Extract (नमुना ८ / नमुना ८ अ)

Official property assessment record extract showing house property valuation, ownership, and annual tax.

Required: House property number, previous tax receipt, Aadhaar.
Official Fee: ₹25 per extract.
💧
7 Working Days

Water Tap Connection (नळ कनेक्शन)

New household piped drinking water connection under Jal Jeevan Mission / Gram Panchayat piped scheme.

Required: House tax receipt (Namuna 8), Identity proof, Site plan sketch.
Security Deposit: ₹500 (Government prescribed).
💍
5 Working Days

Marriage Certificate (विवाह नोंदणी)

Legal marriage registration certificate under Maharashtra Regulation of Marriage Bureaus and Registration of Marriages Act.

Required: Wedding card, 3 witness proofs, Bride & Groom photos & Aadhaar.
Official Fee: ₹50.
🏗️
15 Working Days

Building Permission (बांधकाम परवानगी)

Sanction and NOC for residential house construction or renovation within gaothan / rural area limits.

Required: Blue-print architecture plan, 7/12 extract or Namuna 8, Ownership title.
Fee: Based on sq. footage.
🛡️
3 Working Days

No Objection Certificate (नाहरकत दाखला)

Gram Panchayat NOC for electricity connection (MSEDCL), bank loan, business establishment, or borewell.

Required: Property tax clearance slip, Application stating purpose, ID proof.
Official Fee: ₹20.
🌾
4 Working Days

Ration Card Endorsement (रेशन कार्ड दाखला)

Gram Panchayat family listing endorsement for food & civil supplies ration card inclusion or splitting.

Required: Family register copy, Old ration card, Aadhaar cards of all members.
Official Fee: Free / Nominal ₹10.
🏪
7 Working Days

Trade License (व्यवसाय परवाना)

Rural trade license for grocery stores, dairy collection centers, workshops, and commercial outlets in Valivade.

Required: Shop rent agreement or property extract, Shop act / Udyam registration.
Annual Fee: Based on category schedule.
💡
48 Hours SLA

Streetlight & Civic Repair (नागरी दुरुस्ती)

Request rapid repair of streetlights, water pipeline leaks, road potholes, or drainage blockages.

Action: Routed directly to Panchayat electrical and sanitation staff.
Cost: Free of charge.
Lodge Civic Grievance →
🚜
4 Working Days

Agri Water Supply Slip (शेती पाणी वापर दाखला)

Endorsement certificate for cooperative lift irrigation or canal water quota verification for sugarcane farmers.

Required: 7/12 Land extract showing crop entry, Lift irrigation membership card.
Fee: ₹20.
