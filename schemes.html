Government Welfare & Development Schemes

Central and Maharashtra State Government benefits for farmers, women, youth & rural families

Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) Enabled
Aapla Gaav Citizen Helpdesk:

Gram Panchayat Valivade assists eligible residents in e-KYC, bank account Aadhaar seeding, and application form submission without charge.

Visit Panchayat Desk
🌾
₹6,000 / Year DBT

PM-KISAN Samman Nidhi

Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare

Direct income support of ₹6,000 annually in three equal installments of ₹2,000 transferred directly into the bank accounts of landholding farmer families.

Eligibility: Small & marginal farmers with cultivable land in Valivade.
Required: Aadhaar Card, 7/12 Land extract, Bank Passbook, e-KYC verification.
🏠
₹1.20 Lakh Grant

Pradhan Mantri Awaas Yojana (PMAY-G)

Ministry of Rural Development

Financial grant for the construction of permanent, hygienic pucca houses with clean toilet, drinking water, and electricity connection for homeless rural households.

Eligibility: Houseless families or those living in kutcha/dilapidated homes in SECC list.
Required: Aadhaar, Bank details, Land title/NOC, SECC census ID.
⛏️
100 Days Wage Work

MGNREGA Rural Employment

Mahatma Gandhi NREGA

Legal guarantee of at least 100 days of unskilled manual wage employment every financial year for adult members willing to do public rural works.

Eligibility: Any adult rural citizen residing in Valivade.
Required: MGNREGA Job Card (issued free at Gram Panchayat), Aadhaar, Bank account.
🚰
Har Ghar Jal

Jal Jeevan Mission (JJM)

Department of Drinking Water & Sanitation

Providing individual Functional Household Tap Connections (FHTC) delivering 55 LPCD safe potable water to every rural household in Valivade.

Eligibility: Permanent residents of Valivade without existing connection.
Required: House property extract, Aadhaar card, Application form.
Apply for Connection →
🚽
₹12,000 Incentive

Swachh Bharat Mission (Gramin)

Swachh Bharat Abhiyan

Incentive for individual household sanitary latrines (IHHL) and solid & liquid waste management (SLWM) towards sustaining Valivade's ODF status.

Eligibility: Rural households identified in baseline survey.
Required: Geo-tagged photo of completed toilet, Aadhaar, Bank passbook.
🔥
Free LPG Connection

Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY)

Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas

Clean cooking fuel connection with deposit-free LPG cylinder, pressure regulator, safety hose, and stove subsidy for women from underprivileged families.

Eligibility: Adult woman from BPL/SECC household without active LPG.
Required: Ration Card, Aadhaar of all family members, Bank account.
