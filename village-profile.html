About Valivade (Walivade)

Valivade (वळिवडे), also referenced as Walivade, is a historically significant and progressing village situated in the Karvir (करवीर) Taluka of Kolhapur District, Maharashtra. Located along the fertile Panchganga river basin, the village has direct regional connectivity to Kolhapur city, Hupari, and surrounding commercial hubs.

Valivade holds renowned historical prominence as the site of the large Polish Refugee Camp during World War II (1943–1948), where thousands of Polish refugees found shelter and hospitality under the patronage of the Chhatrapati of Kolhapur. Today, Valivade thrives on agriculture (principally sugarcane and paddy), agro-processing, dairy cooperatives, and expanding residential connectivity.
