Agro-Economy

Agricultural Profile of Valivade

Agriculture is the economic backbone of Valivade (Walivade). Benefiting from the rich, deep black alluvial soil of the Panchganga river basin and favorable climatic conditions, the village produces high-yielding sugarcane, paddy, soybean, and seasonal greens.

Dairy farming forms a critical allied source of rural livelihood, with several dairy cooperative collection centers operating daily in partnership with the acclaimed Kolhapur Zilla Sahakari Dudh Utpadak Sangh (Gokul).

Key Crops & Geography

Primary Cash Crop: Sugarcane (ऊस)
Food Grain Crop: Paddy / Rice (भात)
Oilseeds & Legumes: Soybean, Groundnut
Irrigation Mode: Lift & Canal Basin
Soil Type: Black Alluvial Cotton Soil
Dairy Partner: Gokul Dairy Cooperative

Farming Support & Infrastructure

🚜

Sugarcane Cultivation & Supply

ऊस शेती • Sugar Factory Linkage

Direct tie-ups with cooperative sugar mills in Karvir and neighboring talukas with mechanized harvest transport, cane billing transparency, and drip irrigation adoption.

Major Village Income
🥛

Dairy Cooperatives & Chilling

दुग्ध व्यवसाय • Gokul Dairy Network

Morning and evening computerized milk collection centers with instant FAT/SNF testing, direct bank transfer payments, and veterinary care services for milch cattle.

Allied Rural Employment
📜

PM-KISAN & State Samman Nidhi

शेतकरी सन्मान निधी • ₹12,000 / Year

Combined annual financial assistance of ₹6,000 under PM-KISAN (Centre) and ₹6,000 under Namo Shetkari Mahasanman (Maharashtra) credited directly via DBT.

100% Aadhaar Seeded

Farmer Services & Soil Health Support

Soil Health Card (मृदा आरोग्य पत्रिका)

Laboratory testing of soil N-P-K nutrients and micro-nutrients to reduce fertilizer over-application.

Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima (PMFBY)

Affordable ₹1 crop insurance scheme shielding farmers from erratic rainfall, flood or pest damage.

Solar Agri Pump (PM KUSUM)

Subsidized solar agricultural water pumps ensuring daytime electricity for field irrigation.
