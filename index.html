About Our Village

Fostering Sustainable Growth, Clean Energy & Digital E-Panchayat

Aapla Gaav is a flourishing agricultural and agro-allied village situated in Nagpur Rural Taluka, Maharashtra. Known for its progressive farming communities, orange orchards, and high literacy rate, our village has evolved into a pioneer of digital gram panchayat governance.

Under the Citizen Empowerment Platform (CEP), every rupee of public development funds, ongoing infrastructure projects, and welfare distribution is recorded transparently for citizens and auditors alike.

100% Tap Water

Under Jal Jeevan Mission, every home has functional household tap connectivity.

Green Energy

High-mast solar streetlights illuminate all 5 village wards and farmer roads.

Quality Education

Zilla Parishad High School equipped with smart digital classroom and e-library.

24x7 CSC Center

Citizen Service Kiosk for instant certificates, pensions, and land records.

Direct Citizen Services

Important Village Public Services

Apply for village certificates, revenue extracts, and municipal utility connections seamlessly.

Birth & Death Certificate

Official registration and digital certificate issuance authenticated under the Registration of Births and Deaths Act.

Land Records (7/12 & 8A)

Instant digitally signed Satbara (7/12) and 8A land revenue extracts for agricultural credit, crop loans, and verification.

Water Supply Connection

Request new household tap water connections under the Jal Jeevan Har Ghar Jal mission or report line repairs.

Property Tax (Ghar Patti)

Calculate, review assessment register, and generate official payment challans for annual Gram Panchayat property tax.

Caste & Income Certificate

Gram Sevak verification certificates required for scholarships, educational admissions, and government welfare benefits.

Public Grievance Redressal

Lodge civic complaints concerning roads, sanitation, electricity, water or streetlights directly to the Sarpanch desk.

Welfare & Development

Central & State Government Schemes

Active government welfare flagship programs implemented with 100% transparency in Aapla Gaav.

Housing

Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY-G)

Ministry of Rural Development
Financial grant of ₹1,20,000 + MGNREGA wages for pucca house.

Financial assistance provided to eligible homeless and kutcha-house families in our village for constructing durable concrete homes.

Water

Jal Jeevan Mission (Har Ghar Jal)

Dept of Drinking Water & Sanitation
55 LPCD purified drinking water per person per day.

Aapla Gaav is 100% piped-water compliant. Water quality testing is carried out bi-weekly by village Anganwadi and ASHA workers.

Agriculture

PM-KISAN Samman Nidhi

Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare
Direct cash transfer of ₹6,000 annually in 3 equal installments.

All eligible landholder farmer families in Aapla Gaav receive financial support deposited directly into their Aadhaar-linked bank accounts.

Employment

Mahatma Gandhi NREGA

Rural Employment Guarantee
Guaranteed 100 days wage employment per financial year.

Creating sustainable village infrastructure including lake desilting, tree plantation, concrete farm bunds, and rural farm roads.

Health

Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY)

National Health Authority
Health cover of ₹5,00,000 per family/year for hospitalization.

Free cashless medical treatment in empanelled secondary and tertiary care hospitals across Maharashtra and nationwide.

Renewable

Mukhyamantri Saur Krushi Pump Yojana

Maharashtra State Energy Agency
Up to 90% subsidy on solar water pumps for day-time irrigation.

Assisting farmers with independent, daytime solar irrigation pumps to eliminate reliance on conventional erratic power supply.
