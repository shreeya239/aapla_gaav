Gram Sabha: The Supreme Village Authority

Under the Maharashtra Village Panchayats Act, the Gram Sabha comprises every adult registered voter of Valivade. The Gram Sabha has legal authority to review Panchayat accounts, approve beneficiaries for housing and agricultural schemes, scrutinize village development plans (GPDP), and conduct social audits.
